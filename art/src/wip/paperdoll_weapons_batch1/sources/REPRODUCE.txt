Editable, packed/cleaned scenes in scenes/ reproduce geometry/material/light. Render script can rebuild original meshes with WEAPON_SOURCE_DIR pointing to tools_src/upstream/hero and WEAPON_BATCH_DIR pointing to a new scratch directory. Example: WEAPON_SOURCE_DIR=/absolute/pkg/tools_src/upstream/hero WEAPON_BATCH_DIR=/absolute/scratch blender -b -t 3 --python tools_src/render.py -- m shortsword
finalize.py currently names the local delivery and approved-body paths; remap R and P before running outside the original workspace. paint_helper.py is standalone; dependency Pillow/Numpy/OpenCV. Original public Flare sources retained for derivation/editability, including their historical missing linked material references; delivered scenes have unused linked data purged.
